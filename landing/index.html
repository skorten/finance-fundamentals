Why this course

Finance buyers ask which line moves

When you say "we cut your cloud bill," a CFO hears a different question: which line on which statement moves, and by how much? Cloud and AI spend often sits in cost of revenue, so it shapes gross margin, a number investors watch closely.

This course covers the finance ideas you need for that conversation. It uses a real public SaaS filing, Datadog's FY2025 10-K, as a running example. It covers finance concepts only, with no FinOps content.
