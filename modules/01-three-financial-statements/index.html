Key terms

Six terms you will hear on calls

Income statement
Revenue minus costs over a period. Also called the P&L.
Balance sheet
What the company owns and owes on one date.
Cash flow statement
Cash in and out, split into operating, investing and financing.
10-K
A public company's audited annual report, filed with the SEC.
Accrual accounting
Record revenue and costs when earned or used, not when paid.
Free cash flow
Operating cash flow minus capital spending. A non-GAAP measure.

Part 2

Test yourself on a real 10-K

Open Datadog's FY2025 10-K. Answer three questions.

02

Self-check 1 of 3

What was Datadog's FY2025 gross profit?

  1. A$3,427.2M
  2. B$2,740.2M
  3. C$687.0M

B. $2,740.2M. Revenue of $3,427.2M minus cost of revenue of $687.0M.

Self-check 2 of 3

Did Datadog make an operating profit in FY2025?

  1. AYes, $44.4M profit
  2. BNo, $44.4M loss
  3. CNo, $107.7M loss

B. A $44.4M loss. Operating expenses of $2,784.6M beat gross profit of $2,740.2M.

Self-check 3 of 3

Which was larger in FY2025: net income or operating cash flow?

  1. ANet income
  2. BOperating cash flow
  3. CThey were equal

B. $1,050.1M against $107.7M. Stock pay and cash collected early explain most of the gap.
