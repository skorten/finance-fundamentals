Key terms

Six terms you will hear every call

COGS
Direct cost of delivering what you sold, including production cloud.
Gross profit
Revenue minus cost of revenue. What is left to fund the business.
Gross margin
Gross profit divided by revenue, shown as a percent.
OpEx
Costs of running the business outside delivery, like sales and G&A.
R&D
Research and development. Dev and test cloud often lands here.
Basis point
One hundredth of a point. 80.0% to 80.5% is 50 basis points.

Self-check 1 of 3

Datadog FY2025: revenue $3,427.2M, gross profit $2,740.2M. What is gross margin?

  1. A20.0%
  2. B80.0%
  3. C125.0%

B. 80.0%. $2,740.2M ÷ $3,427.2M. FY2024 was 80.8%, so margin fell about 80 basis points.

Self-check 2 of 3

Hypothetical: Datadog avoids the $149.8M hosting increase, all else equal. FY2025 gross margin becomes?

  1. A80.0%, no change
  2. B82.0%
  3. C84.3%

C. 84.3%. Cost of revenue drops to $537.2M, gross profit rises to $2,890.0M. A what-if, not a savings estimate.

Self-check 3 of 3

Which cost usually lands in operating expenses, not COGS?

  1. AProduction cloud compute
  2. BAI calls inside the product
  3. CEngineering test environment

C. Test cloud usually books to R&D. Policies vary, so ask the controller how they classify it.
