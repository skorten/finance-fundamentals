Self-check 1 · Illustrative

A company capitalizes $600K of development labor. It amortizes straight-line over three years. What expense hits each year?

  1. A$100K
  2. B$200K
  3. C$600K

B. $200K. $600K ÷ 3 = $200K a year. After year 1 the asset is worth $400K. Expensing it would add $400K to year 1.

Key terms

Six terms you will hear on calls

Capex
Spending on long lived assets, expensed gradually over their useful life.
Opex
Spending expensed in the period it happens, like a monthly cloud bill.
Capitalization
Recording a cost as an asset instead of expensing it now.
Amortization
Spreading a software asset's cost over its useful life, evenly if straight-line.
ASC 350-40
US GAAP rules for software built or bought to run your own business.
ASU 2025-06
FASB update that changes when capitalizing internal-use software starts.

Self-check 2 · Hypothetical

A company capitalizes $90M at the start of the year. How much higher is first-year operating income with a three year life than a two year life?

  1. A$15M
  2. B$30M
  3. C$45M

A. $15M. Two years is $45M a year. Three years is $30M. Total amortization stays $90M, spread over an extra year.

Self-check 3

Which cost is possibly capitalizable, depending on company policy and finance's call?

  1. AMonthly production cloud bill
  2. BBug fixes in a shipped feature
  3. CSalaries on an approved, funded feature

C. The cloud bill is opex. Bug fixes are usually expensed. Datadog capitalizes costs like C. Finance decides.
