Unit economics

Finance wants profit on each customer

  • Revenue comes in every month
  • Cost to serve goes out: hosting and AI
  • What is left is gross profit

The formula

One customer's margin uses that customer's cost

Margin = ( Revenue − Cost to serve ) ÷ Revenue

Worked example · Illustrative

($10K − $4K) ÷ $10K = 60%

Key terms

Six terms you will hear on every call

Unit cost
Total cost for a period divided by the number of units.
Cost to serve
What it costs to deliver the service to one customer.
Customer margin
Customer revenue minus their cost to serve, divided by revenue.
Contribution margin
Revenue minus variable costs, the ones that move with usage.
CAC
Sales and marketing spend needed to win one new customer.
LTV
Monthly gross profit times expected months. Match definitions before comparing.

Self-check 1 of 2

A customer pays $10K a month and costs $4K to serve. AI usage doubles, price stays flat. New gross margin?

  1. A50%
  2. B45%
  3. C30%

B. 45%. AI cost rises to $3K, so cost to serve is $5.5K. ($10K − $5.5K) ÷ $10K = 45%.

Self-check 2 of 2

Three customers share a $30K cluster. Prices: A $14K, B $12K, C $10K. Usage: 50%, 30%, 20%. Who loses money?

  1. ACustomer A
  2. BCustomer B
  3. CCustomer C

A. A uses $15K of cost against $14K of revenue. B earns 25%, C earns 40%. The blend, 16.7%, hides it.
