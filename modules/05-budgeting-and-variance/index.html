Core idea

The budget stays put. The forecast moves.

  • Budget: the plan, fixed before the year
  • Actuals: what happened, after close
  • Forecast: the best guess for the rest

Key terms

Six terms you will hear in every review

Budget
The annual plan, set before the fiscal year starts.
Forecast
The updated expectation during the year. It moves.
Actuals
What really happened, as recorded after close.
Variance
Actual minus budget, for one line and one period.
Favorable
Helps profit. Unfavorable hurts it. Costs over budget are unfavorable.

Self-check

A team budgeted $500K for cloud and AI and spent $572K. What is the variance?

  1. A$72K, 14.4% unfavorable
  2. B$72K, 14.4% favorable
  3. C$72K, 12.6% unfavorable

A. $572K − $500K = $72K. Divide by the $500K budget for 14.4%. A cost over budget is unfavorable.
