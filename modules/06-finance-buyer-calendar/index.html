Key terms

Six terms you will hear every call

Fiscal year
A company's 12 month accounting year. It may not match the calendar year.
Close
Working days after a period ends when accounting finalizes that period's books.
Audit
The external auditor's examination of the annual financial statements.
SOX
The 2002 US law setting internal control and certification rules for public companies.
Internal controls
Processes that make reported numbers reliable. SOX 404 requires a yearly assessment.
Budget cycle
The months before the year starts, when next year's budget gets set.

Showback vs chargeback

Showback informs. Chargeback bills the budget.

Showback

Report each team's share. Nobody pays.

vs

Chargeback

Charge each team's budget for its share.

Self-check 1 of 2

Datadog's fiscal year ends December 31. Using the two to four month rule, when is its budget season?

  1. ASeptember to year end
  2. BFebruary to April
  3. CJune and July

A. September to year end. This is an inference, not in the 10-K. Confirm timing with the prospect.

Self-check 2 of 2

A hypothetical $72K budget overrun needs explaining by team. Which role owns that question first?

  1. ACFO
  2. BController
  3. CFP&A

C. FP&A. It is a variance question. The CFO owns margin and story. The controller owns accuracy and audit.
