The formula

Gross margin shows what delivery really costs

Gross margin = ( Revenue − COGS ) ÷ Revenue

Worked example

($10.0M − $2.8M) ÷ $10.0M = 72%

COGS here is $1.8M cloud hosting plus $1.0M support.

Key terms

Six terms you will hear every call

COGS
Costs to deliver what you sold, including production cloud.
Gross margin
Share of revenue left after paying the cost to deliver.
OpEx
Costs to run the business, like R&D, sales and admin.
Accrual
Recording a cost when incurred, even before the invoice arrives.
Capex
Spend on long lived assets, expensed slowly through depreciation.
Unit cost
What it costs to serve one customer, transaction or feature.

Self-check

A SaaS company moves dev and test cloud spend out of COGS. What happens to gross margin?

  1. AIt goes up
  2. BIt goes down
  3. CNo change

A. It goes up. The cost moves to R&D OpEx, so gross margin rises while operating income stays the same.
